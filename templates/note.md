## Key takeaway

What the source supports, in your own words.

## Concepts and frameworks

Definitions, assumptions, and limits. Separate source material from interpretation.

## Application

How this could change a real business decision. Label hypothetical examples.

## My reflection

Use only reflections provided by the learner; otherwise mark as awaiting input.

## Questions to revisit

Unresolved claims, unfamiliar terms, and follow-up reading.

## Source notes

Cite the URL, title, author/date when available, and page/slide or section for important claims.
