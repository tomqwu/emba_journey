# Learning-method research and implementation

Reviewed 2026-10-04 for a personal bilingual EMBA knowledge base. The objective is to improve recall, explanation, and application of sourced ideas. No universal framework ranking or EMBA efficacy claim is made.

## Selection and boundaries

Retrieval and spacing are the strongest general retention priorities in this selected evidence set. Self-explanation adds understanding prompts. Kolb supplies an application scaffold with a different evidence status. Diagrams and connected notes remain explanatory/navigation tools; the retrieval experiment does not justify removing them. Reading and highlighting alone do not implement retrieval. No learning-style matching, unsupported retention percentages, or mastery badges are introduced.

This is a focused evidence review, not a systematic review of all frameworks. “Best” is interpreted as best fit for the user's goals and current site. Findings from school/university contexts do not directly establish executive decision improvements.

## Site mapping

- `learning-how-to-learn.md`, in both languages: evidence, sources, limits, and a learning-loop diagram.
- `learning-practice` Markdown fences: learning objective, stable recall/explain/transfer questions, suggested answers, and a workplace application prompt.
- `learn.html`, in both languages: due/new queue, answer attempt, feedback reveal, self-ratings, related source note, and an experience/reflection/experiment journal.
- Browser storage: shared language-independent review keys and journal entries; Markdown export. Personal responses never enter source Markdown automatically.
- Source-question revisions: changed prompts or answers reset eligibility for review without destroying the previous exportable response.

## Scheduling assumptions

The schedule is a product heuristic: again → 1 day; partial → 3 days; initial clear recall → 7 days; subsequent clear recall → 14/30/60 days, capped at 60. Toronto calendar days avoid timezone-dependent due dates. It is not SM-2, FSRS, a calibrated forgetting model, or an evidence-based optimal interval. Self-ratings are not objective correctness or mastery. Due items appear when the learner opens the site; there are no background reminders.

## Validation
